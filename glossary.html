Glossary

Terms that appear in the program and in this documentation.

TermMeaning
Body / OutlineTwo roles of a pixel: body (fill) and outline. Each has its own colors, chance and an active flag.
ChanceThe probability (0-100%) that a pixel or layer is used by the generator.
AutoColorThe pixel color is picked from the palette automatically.
CellA layer in a particular frame. The Move tool without a selection moves the whole cell.
FrameOne moment of an animation; up to 120 frames per document; numbered from 1 in the interface.
Tag (animation tag)A named range of frames (idle, walk). It becomes a clip when exported to an atlas.
Onion skinA translucent display of neighboring frames (up to 4 before and 4 after).
InbetweenAn automatic intermediate pose between two frames.
Layer groupA flat group of layers with shared visibility and locking.
Alpha maskA layer that uses the alpha channel of another layer as a mask, optionally inverted.
Blend modeA layer blend mode: Normal, Multiply, Screen, Add.
Add-onA generator mode (Effects, Furniture, UI, Character) that is enabled on top of Drawing with the "Apply ... to Drawing" checkbox.
SeedA number that determines the randomness; the same seed and settings give the same result.
VariationA variant of settings (for example a noise profile) or a result created from a selected sprite.
IndexedA document whose pixels are bound to palette slots; changing the palette changes the whole animation.
RampA row of shades from shadow to highlight built from a base color.
DitheringA pattern of pixels of two colors that imitates intermediate tones.
SliceA named rectangular area with a pivot and a 9-slice center.
PivotThe anchor point of a slice relative to its bounds.
9-sliceSplitting an image into 9 parts: the corners stay unchanged, the center stretches.
AtlasA sprite sheet with a description of frames and clips (Unity, Godot, Phaser).
Tile RepeatA mode in which the drawing wraps around the canvas edges.
.sfThe SpriteForge project format.

Next-edit prediction

Suggestions appear after a pause in drawing: the program offers to continue a contour, repeat a pattern, mirror an edit or transfer it to another frame. Everything is computed locally from geometry, with no neural network and no network access.

How to use it

  • The feature is on by default. The Predict next edit checkbox is in Properties > General; transferring edits between frames is turned on there too.
  • After a stroke ends and a pause of about 350 ms, a translucent version of the edit appears on the canvas with a teal frame and a caption.
  • Tab accepts the suggestion (when the canvas has focus or the cursor is over it), Esc dismisses it. Shift+Tab - the next suggestion. New drawing removes the suggestion.
  • Accepting is one Undo step. The preview does not change the document, the history or the export.

What is predicted

SuggestionWhen it appears
Continue contourtwo adjacent points of the same color give a one-pixel suggestion; further points refine the slope
Complete rectanglethree sides of a thin axis-aligned outline suggest the fourth
Continue arcat least five ordered points of an arc with a noticeable bend
Continue direction patternrepeating cycles of directions ("right - down - right") and pixel steps
Repeat patternthree identical strokes or stamps with an equal step suggest a fourth
Repeat grid patterna regular grid of elements of at least 2 x 2 with a single empty spot
Mirror horizontally / verticallythe drawing is symmetric about an axis by at least 90% (at least three pairs): the last edit is carried to the paired place
Apply edit to similar drawingsimilar figures exist on the canvas (by shape and colors): the edit is offered for them too; Shift+Tab picks another figure
Transfer to adjacent framethe same fragment is found in the previous or next frame (offset search up to ±12 pixels, at least a 94% match): the edit is shown enlarged with the frame number and the offset

Limitations

  • Prediction works on canvases up to 262144 pixels and for strokes up to 128 changed pixels.
  • After accepting a contour, arc or pattern, the next continuation of the same action is prepared immediately; pressing Tab again accepts it as a separate Undo step.
  • Body/Outline, Chance, AutoColor and the palette index of the source pixels are preserved.
  • Pixels removed by a transfer are marked with a red diagonal.

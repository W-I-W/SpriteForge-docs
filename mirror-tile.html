Mirror and Tile Repeat

Two modes that repeat your actions automatically: symmetry and seamless tile repetition.

Mirror (symmetry)

Choose the Mirror tool in the Tools panel (in family 5 by holding the button) - symmetry turns on automatically. The settings are in the Tool settings window while the tool is active:

  • Canvas symmetry - the main switch (the checkbox at the left of the row).
  • Mode: symmetry applies to drawing ( During drawing), to generation ( During generation) or to both - the icons switch independently and apply to all axes.
  • Axes - four independent buttons: Left / right (vertical axis), Top / bottom (horizontal axis), Diagonal: top left to bottom right, Diagonal: bottom left to top right. The tooltip shows ON/OFF; any combination can be turned on, including all four.
  • Show mirror lines - show the axes on the canvas.
  • Center (px) - the exact position of the center (0.5 px step); Center in canvas returns the center to the middle of the canvas.

The center can also be moved with the mouse: with the Mirror tool active, a handle with coordinates ("Mirror x, y px") is visible on the canvas - drag it. Esc exits the tool. Subpixel reflections are rasterized to the pixel grid, and pixels outside the canvas are clipped.

How the mirror source is chosen

The highlighted area is the preferred source: the topmost pixels first, then the leftmost. Already painted pixels take priority. While drawing, copies are built from the cursor.

An axis from a guide

Any guide can be turned into a mirror axis: right-click the line → Use as mirror (in the Guides window - Mirror for generation). Such lines are colored green on the canvas; the selected guide is orange.

Mirror during generation

If the During generation icon is on, generated sprites are reflected around the same axes and the generator's work area shrinks. The drawing itself is not changed. There is no longer a separate "Generator mirror" section with its own axes - the shared canvas axes are used.

Tile Repeat

The Tile Repeat settings window (available from the window menu of the Drawing mode):

  • Enable Tile Repeat - turn repetition on. Painting wraps around the canvas edges: what leaves on the right appears on the left, and vice versa.
  • Horizontal tiles / Vertical tiles - the number of tile repetitions in the preview along each axis.

Tile Repeat is the main way to draw seamless textures: look at the seams in the preview window, not only at the canvas itself. For an automatic set of 48 tiles use the Tiles generator.
